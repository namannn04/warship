# Visible crew blockout

Two gunners and a carpenter now have physical blockout actors on the gun deck. They move in ship-local coordinates toward their claimed cannon or repair station, remain carried by the ship, and only progress a job once they arrive. Role labels identify them during testing.

These are intentionally simple geometry and movement. They do not yet use skeletal animation, collision-aware navigation, spoken responses, morale, or the 20–40 visible sailors described in the final brief. That work needs Unreal runtime testing and character assets.
