# Storage hold traversal blockout

The gun deck now has a second real hatch, offset forward from the weather-deck hatch. Ten physical steps lead from the gun deck to a third, lower hold level. The hull walls enclose both interior levels; six supply crates line the hold sides while leaving the middle route open. No teleport or level load is used between these spaces.

This is a structural blockout. In Unreal, verify that the captain can descend both flights and return while the ship moves, and that the camera does not clip through the low ceilings. The hold crates are visual supply markers; dynamic cargo placement is still future work.
