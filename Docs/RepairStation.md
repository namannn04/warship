# Physical hull repair and bilge pump

A repair station sits on the lower gun deck near the stern. The captain can walk down the open stairs, look at the station and press **E**. When a hull breach exists, each action patches 10 severity and consumes two planks. When the breach is closed but water remains, each action pumps 10 water. An undamaged ship reports a dry bilge.

This is manual emergency work. Crew task assignment, visible water, repair animations and emergency orders are future steps. The repair and pump rules have an isolated C++ test; the physical station still needs Unreal runtime verification.
